About Me

Blue collar roots. Operator mindset.

My name is Jeff Stark. As a teenager, one of my first jobs was working for a general contractor — doing everything from demolition to roof framing. It was hard, honest work, and it shaped how I see the world.

That blue-collar start gave me a deep appreciation for service businesses and the people who build them. I saw firsthand the pride owners take in their work and the trust their customers and employees place in them.

For the past 15 years I've led digital marketing teams at agencies, as an independent consultant advising small businesses, and most recently on the brand marketing team at one of the largest Japanese automotive OEM's in North America. I've seen what works, what doesn't, and how good operators build lasting businesses.

I founded nTrusted Co. in 2024 with my wife, with one clear goal: acquire one exceptional small business, step in as a hands-on operator, and build something that lasts — for employees, customers, and community.
